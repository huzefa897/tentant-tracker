# Property dashboard

The dashboard has two lists:

- **Rent due** (the default), for the selected property: which months' rent is due or overdue, whether each one's bill is prepared, and how much is known to be outstanding. A month is due on the tenancy's schedule whether or not its bill exists ([rent-entries.md](rent-entries.md#rental-periods-and-due-dates)). This is where a month without a bill shows up.
- **Overdue bills** (`?view=overdue`): which tenants have bills past their due date with money still to pay, across every property the user has, grouped by tenant, so the owners know whom to remind. Only bills: a month without a bill is never treated as paid or owed here.

## Overdue bills

- **What counts:** active bills whose due date is before today (in India, `business_date()`) with an outstanding balance above zero. A bill is due when its rental period is (`RentEntryQuerySet.with_due`), so one prepared late keeps its month's due date. Paid, void and not-yet-due bills (including due today) are left out. Amounts are the one SQL definition (below): payments that weren't reversed, so a reversal makes a bill overdue again and a payment that settles it takes it off the list, the same figures as the bill page.
- **API:** `GET /api/v1/overdue-bills/` across the user's properties (`properties_for_user`), `billing/overdue.py`. `asOfDate`, `generatedAt`, `currency`, `tenantCount`, `billCount`, `totalOutstandingAmount` (of the filtered list) and `tenants`: `tenancyId`, `name`, `flatNumber`, `tenancyStatus`, `propertyId`, `propertyName`, `overdueBillCount`, `outstandingAmount`, `oldestDueDate` and `bills` (`id`, `billingMonth`, `dueDate`, `daysOverdue`, `totalAmount`, `paidAmount`, `outstandingAmount`), oldest due first. Tenants with the oldest overdue bill come first (then name, flat, property). `?property=<id>` (one of the user's; anything else is 400 `property`, never a hint another exists), `?month=YYYY-MM` (billing month), `?search=` (tenant name or flat, case-insensitive; "Flat 102" finds 102; at most 100 characters). Not paged: one group per tenancy that owes. Its query count doesn't grow with bills (tested).
- **App:** `DashboardView` shows it at `?view=overdue` (rent due is the default). `dashboardService.getOverdueBills` validates the response (a tenant's bills match its count, each is overdue with money left), `stores/overdue.ts` holds it with its filters; it spans properties, so it resets on sign-out but not when the selected property changes. **Search tenant or flat** (sent once typing pauses, or on Enter), **Property** (only with more than one) and **Month** (the last two years), with **Clear filters**. The totals line (₹ outstanding · tenants · overdue bills), then a card per tenant (`OverdueTenantList`): name, property (with several) and flat, the total outstanding and how many bills, then each month with its due date, days overdue ("Overdue 35 days", worded like Rent due's status), total, paid and balance (fixed column widths, so the columns line up from card to card) (a table from `sm` up, tappable rows on phones). **View bill** opens the bill (`?from=dashboard`); the tenant's name opens their bills. A bill in another property selects that property first, since the bill pages work within the selected one.
- **States:** refreshed whenever it's shown and from **Refresh**, with **Updated 2 minutes ago**. A failed first load is an error with Try again, never "No overdue bills"; a failed refresh keeps the list and says **Couldn’t refresh**. A filter change replaces the list with skeleton rows and, if it fails, an error with Try again in its place: the old filters' list is never shown as the new one's. **No overdue bills.** when there are none; **No overdue bills match your filters.** with Clear filters when the filters leave nothing.

## Rent due: what counts

- **Rows are rental periods**, one per tenancy and month, never bills: a month without a bill is still due or overdue. Months count within the tenancy's dates, from its first month (or the property's tracking cut-off, `Property.rent_tracking_start`, when it has a later one: [rent-entries.md](rent-entries.md#rental-periods-and-due-dates)); a month with a bill always counts. Months marked **not owed** never do.
- **Amounts come only from prepared bills.** Active bills, with the payments that weren't reversed; void bills count for nothing. A month awaiting its bill has no amount: it's `null` in the API, never `"0.00"`, and it's left out of every sum (the summary counts how many). Nothing is estimated, not even the rent.
- **One definition** of a bill's amounts in SQL (`billing/models.py`: `effective_paid_sql`, `total_due_sql`, `outstanding_sql`), used by bill lists, their filters (`RentEntry.objects.with_amounts()`, `.owing()`) and the dashboard (`RentalPeriod.objects.with_bill()`, `.counted()`, `.owing()`). Payments are summed in a subquery per bill, never joined, so nothing is counted once per payment.
- **Timing**, on today in India (`business_date()`, once per request) against the period's saved due date: **overdue** (due date before today, with the days overdue), **due today**, **upcoming** (tomorrow to a week from today). No grace period.
- **Settlement**: **unknown** (no bill), then **unpaid**, **partially paid** or **paid** from the bill's effective payments. A reversal makes a month owed again, from its original due date.
- **Periods are created by the reads themselves** (`periods.ensure_property_periods`, at the start of every dashboard request), so a day of downtime or no visits never hides a month. No scheduled job.
- Everything is counted and summed in the database across the whole property before paging (`billing/dashboard.py`); the list's filters never change the summary.

## Rent due: API

Reads are for anyone with access to the property (`properties_for_user`; another property 404s). Money is a decimal string.

- `GET /api/v1/properties/{id}/dashboard/summary/`: `asOfDate`, `generatedAt`, `currency`; counts of **rental periods**: `owedPeriodCount` (every month still owed, due or not yet), `overduePeriodCount`, `oldestOverdueDays` (how long the oldest overdue month is past due; `null` when none is), `dueTodayPeriodCount`, `upcomingPeriodCount`, `awaitingBillPeriodCount` (owed months with no bill, left out of the amounts), `overdueAwaitingBillPeriodCount`; counts of **tenancies**: `overdueTenancyCount`, `owingTenancyCount`; and amounts known from prepared bills: `knownOutstandingAmount`, `knownOverdueAmount`, `knownDueTodayAmount`, `knownUpcomingAmount`.
- `GET .../dashboard/rental-periods/`: one row per month still owed or awaiting its bill, oldest due first (then name, flat, month): `id`, `tenancyId`, `name`, `flatNumber`, `tenancyStatus`, `periodMonth` (`YYYY-MM`), `paymentTiming`, `dueDate`, `timing`, `daysOverdue`, `preparation` (`prepared` | `not_prepared`), `billId`, `settlement`, `totalDue` and `outstandingAmount` (both `null` without a bill), and `actions`: `prepare_bill` and `mark_not_owed` (owners) without a bill, or `view_bill` and `record_payment` with one, only those the viewer's role allows. `?timing=due` (the default: overdue or due today) `|overdue|due_today|upcoming|all`, `?preparation=all|prepared|not_prepared`, `?month=YYYY-MM`; 20 a page (`?page=`). Its query count doesn't grow with months, bills or payments (tested).
- `POST /api/v1/properties/{id}/rental-periods/{periodId}/not-owed/` `{"reason"}`: marks a month not owed (waived, or settled outside the app). Owners only (403 for a manager), 201 with the period and who, when and why; a retry with the same reason is 200, another reason 409 `not_owed_conflict`; a month with an active bill is 409 `period_has_bill` (void the bill first). It can't have a bill afterwards.
- **A tenancy's unpaid bills:** the bills list takes `?balance=outstanding` (active bills with money still to pay), with the rest of its filters and paging ([rent-entries.md](rent-entries.md)).

## Rent due: app

After signing in and choosing a property, the dashboard (`DashboardView`) opens on **Rent due**, the first toggle; **Overdue bills** is the second (`?view=overdue`). Choosing a tenant in the toolbar search shows their details as before, with a button back to the list. Rent due is the selected property's, the one chosen in the app's property switch.

- **Data:** `services/dashboard.service.ts` validates each response (money as two-decimal strings or, for a month without a bill, `null`; a prepared month must have its bill and amounts and one without a bill must have neither; dates, months, counts, statuses and actions) and maps it to `types/dashboard.ts`. `stores/dashboard.ts` holds the summary and one page of months for the chosen filters (`timing`, `preparation`, `month`). It resets as soon as the property changes.
- **Refreshing:** whenever Rent due is shown (opening it, coming back from a bill, payment, reversal or correction, another property) and from **Refresh**. A refresh loads the summary and the list together and replaces them only when both arrive; a refresh while one is running waits for it. **Updated 2 minutes ago** moves on by itself.
- **Failures:** a failed first load is an error with Try again (auto-retried for passing errors), never zeros. A failed refresh keeps everything shown and says **Couldn’t refresh. Showing data from 6:30 pm.**; nothing signs the user out. Changing a filter or page reloads only the list, with skeleton rows in its place and its own error and Try again.
- **Header:** **Rent due** with the selected property's name beside it, and **Updated 2 minutes ago** with Refresh on the right.
- **Summary** (`DashboardSummary`): one strip of three, stacked on phones and side by side from `lg`, each a term (hidden) and value (`dl`) under a hidden **Summary** heading. **Overdue**: "2 months overdue · Oldest 35 days · ₹5,750 billed" (the amount only from prepared bills), tinted red when any, else **Nothing overdue**. **Bills**: "2 bills not prepared · 1 of them overdue" with **Show them** (every owed month without a bill: All owed, Bill: Not prepared), else **Every bill prepared**. **Outstanding**: the known outstanding from prepared bills with how many tenants owe and the months awaiting a bill it leaves out ("₹5,750 outstanding · 2 tenants · not counting 2 months awaiting a bill"); **No amount yet** when no owed month has a bill, never ₹0; **Nothing owed** when nothing is. Due today and the next 7 days are counted on the tabs. Counts of months are never called tenants; only overdue months take colour.
- **The list** (`PeriodFilterBar`, `RentalPeriodTable`), in one card under a hidden **Rental months** heading: native selects for **Bill** (All, Prepared, Not prepared) and **Month** (the last 12), with **Clear filters**, on the same row as the tab-like toggle buttons from `lg` up (above them on phones): for when it's due (**Due now**, the default: overdue or due today; Overdue; Due today; Next 7 days; All owed), each with how many months it has from the summary (All owed from `owedPeriodCount`), read out as "(3 months)". The counts are hidden while Bill or Month narrows the list, since the summary doesn't change with them. The buttons wrap on phones rather than scroll sideways. A table from `lg` up (Tenant, Flat, Month, Due date, Outstanding, Status, actions; the tenant is each row's header and a hidden caption names the filters, e.g. "Rental months due now (overdue or due today), awaiting a bill, oldest due first"), cards below. Outstanding is what's left to pay, with "of ₹15,750" under it once part is paid; a month without a bill says **Bill not prepared** there, never ₹0. A row has at most two actions, one outlined and one plain. The status (`PeriodStatusBadge`) is words, never colour alone: **Overdue 25 days** (red), **Due today** (amber), **Due in 3 days** (grey), outlined rather than tinted so the text keeps AA contrast. Pages of 20.
- **Actions**, only those the row's `actions` allow: **Prepare bill** opens the new-bill form on that month (`?month=YYYY-MM`; the bill is due on the month's date, however late it's made); **View bill** opens the bill (`?from=dashboard`), and **Record payment** opens it with Record payment already open (`?pay=1`), where payments are recorded against that bill as usual. **Not owed** (owners) asks why (`NotOwedForm`, in a dialog that stays open while saving and keeps the reason on failure), then refreshes, so the month leaves the list and the counts together.
- **Tests:** the dashboard is where sign-in lands, so `src/test/setup.ts` mocks `dashboard.service` with `src/test/mocks/dashboardService.ts` for every test ([testing.md](testing.md)).
