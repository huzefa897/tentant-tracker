# Electricity meter readings

A tenancy's electricity can be measured from its meter instead of typed in as units. Readings, bills and payments are kept apart: recording a reading never creates, changes or settles a bill, a bill can leave electricity off, and rent can be paid without a reading.

## Baseline (admin)

Admin (Tenancies → **Electricity meter**, `TenancyAdminForm`): **Enable meter reading tracking**, the **Initial meter reading** (`Tenancy.meter_baseline_reading`: the move-in reading, or the meter's reading now for an existing tenancy) and **Baseline recorded at** (`meter_baseline_recorded_at`, read-only). Ticked needs the reading; unticked clears it. The time is never typed in: `Tenancy.save()` sets it to now whenever the reading is entered or changed, and clears it with the reading (a time the caller sets, as tests do, is kept). It only does so when the save writes the baseline: `save(update_fields=[...])` without it stamps nothing and makes no extra query. Both or neither (check constraint `tenancy_meter_baseline_complete`). **Empty means tracking is off**, never zero: the tenancy's bills take typed units, as before. Nothing earlier is reinterpreted: every bill and receipt from before is `manual`.

The day it was recorded (`Tenancy.meter_baseline_date()`, at the property) is where the meter starts: readings are dated from that day on, and a meter bill from the baseline opens on it. It isn't printed on receipts.

Once the tenancy has an active reading, tracking stays on and the initial reading is fixed (`Tenancy.clean()`, and `save()` under the row lock that recording a reading takes, `TenancyHasReadingsError`; admin shows both read-only). Turning tracking off and on again would leave readings and unbilled units with nothing to measure from, so it isn't offered: void the readings in the app first. Once every reading is void the baseline can change; the meter then starts again from the new reading and the day it was recorded, and the void readings stay in the list as history, measuring nothing. The tenancy API returns the baseline as `meterBaseline: {reading, readOn}` (`readOn`: the day it was recorded) or `null`.

## Readings

`billing.MeterReading`: tenancy, reading (≥ 0, three decimals), `read_on` (the day it was taken: the bill form defaults it to the initial payment's date, else today), notes, creator, an idempotency key and fingerprint, and the bill-style lifecycle (`status` active | void, `voided_*`, `replaces_reading`). One active reading per tenancy and day. Never edited or deleted: view-only in admin, and the database refuses it too (trigger `billing_meterreading_guard`, `billing.0014`): an `UPDATE` can't change what was read, when, by whom or under which key, or un-void a reading; a `DELETE` is refused; a replacement must replace a _void_ reading of the _same tenancy_. Only the active → void change is allowed. (`TRUNCATE` doesn't fire row triggers.)

`billing/meter.py` `record_meter_reading` (owners and managers, `Capability.RECORD_READINGS`) locks the tenancy `FOR UPDATE` (bill creation's lock), then checks: the meter is set up; the date is within the tenancy, not before the day the baseline was recorded and not after `business_date()`; the value isn't below the baseline, below the active reading before it or above the one after it (so a backdated reading must fit between its neighbours). Readings never go down: **a meter replaced or reset isn't supported yet**. A missed month needs nothing: the next reading covers it, and no reading is ever invented.

**Consumption** (units since the active reading before, or the baseline) is derived for the list, never stored.

**Units instead of the reading.** A new reading can be entered as the units used since the latest reading (`units` instead of `reading`, exactly one): `meter.reading_from_units`, under the tenancy's lock, adds them to the **newest active reading** (else the baseline), whether billed or not, and the result passes the same order checks. So it doesn't matter, month to month, whether the tenant gives the meter's figure or the units. Units are dated after that reading (or on the baseline's day when there's none yet): a backdated reading, between two others, is entered as the reading itself. The reading keeps `entered_units` (null for a typed reading; never edited, `billing.0017` adds it to the guard trigger), shown in the history as "Entered as 109 units": such a reading is a running total and may not match the dial, and a real reading entered later absorbs any difference in its consumption. A replaced or reset meter still isn't supported, but units keep counting past one. The idempotency fingerprint is the request (the units), not the reading they make, so a retry after later readings still replays. A correction always takes the reading itself (`units` is refused there). A tenancy without a baseline has no running total: its bills take typed units as before; to track one by units only, set its initial meter reading to 0 in admin at a billing boundary (units before the baseline's day aren't carried into the first meter bill).

## Bills

`RentEntry.electricity_mode`:

| Mode          | When                                                           | Units                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `manual`      | No baseline, electricity included (and every bill from before) | Typed in                                     |
| `meter`       | Baseline, electricity included                                 | Closing reading − last billed reading        |
| `not_charged` | Electricity left off (`includeElectricity: false`)             | 0; the readings wait for the next meter bill |

A meter bill saves `closing_reading`, `opening_reading` (null when it starts from the baseline) and `meter_opening`/`meter_opening_date` (the opening's figures when it was created, so a later baseline change never changes it), and stores its units in `electricity_units` like any bill: totals, balances, the dashboard and receipts are worked out as before.

- **Last billed reading**: the closing reading of the tenancy's latest active meter bill, else the baseline. A meter bill always starts there. A `not_charged` bill doesn't move it, so October billed at 2563, November read at 2876 but not charged, December billed at 2985 charges **422** units. **The whole stretch is charged at the bill's rate**, the tenancy's electricity rate copied when the bill is created, even if the rate changed during the months it covers; the bill form shows the units and rate before saving.
- **Each stretch is billed once.** Conditional unique constraints on active bills (`unique_active_entry_closing_reading`, `unique_active_entry_opening_reading`, `unique_active_meter_entry_from_baseline`) and the tenancy's lock keep the active meter bills one chain from the baseline. The closing reading must be after the last billed one by day and not below it in value (400 on `closingReading`); the same figure on a later day is a meter bill of 0 units, not "Not charged". Triggers (`billing.0016`) keep a bill on its own tenancy's readings, active while the bill is, and refuse voiding a reading an active bill opens or closes on. They are a backstop for a script or shell session that doesn't take the tenancy's lock (the two lock the reading between them, so one waits for the other's commit); `billing/meter.py` is what does the work.
- Creating a bill (`create_rent_entry` → `meter.electricity_for_new_bill`): `closingReading {reading | units, readOn, notes?}` records a new reading (units are added to the newest reading, as above; the bill still charges from the last billed one) in the same transaction (rolled back with the bill; 400 on `closingReading` without one), or `closingReadingId` picks an unbilled one. A baseline tenancy's typed units are refused; a tenancy without one refuses readings. With electricity left off, a reading sent is recorded as a reading only.
- **Voiding** a meter bill frees its stretch for the next meter bill. While a later active meter bill starts from its closing reading it's refused (409 `later_meter_bill`, with that bill): void newest first. It takes the tenancy's lock (`NO KEY`, after the bill's).
- **Correcting** a bill keeps its mode and readings (rent, water and notes change). A meter or not-charged bill's units can't change there (400 on `electricityUnits`): void it and create a new bill.
- Bills in the API carry `electricityMode`, `openingReading` and `closingReading` (`{id, reading, readOn}`, the opening's id null for the baseline, or null).

## Corrections

`void_meter_reading` and `correct_meter_reading` (owners and managers; reason, and for a correction an Idempotency-Key) lock the tenancy, then the reading. **A reading an active bill opens or closes on is refused** (409 `reading_in_use`): `entries` lists every bill to void first, newest first, which is those bills and every meter bill after them (`bills_to_void_for`). Reverse their payments, void them in that order, correct the reading, then bill again. No saved amount ever changes underneath a bill. A replacement passes the same order checks against the other readings.

## Receipts

`Receipt.electricity_mode`, `opening_reading`, `opening_read_on`, `closing_reading`, `closing_read_on`, copied from the bill when issued (old receipts are `manual` with none). A bill that opens on the baseline has no `opening_read_on`: the day tracking was switched on isn't printed. In the API under `charges`: `electricityMode`, `openingReading`, `closingReading` (`{reading, readOn}` or null; the opening's `readOn` is null when it's the baseline).

## App

- **Types and service:** `types/meterReading.ts`; `services/meterReading.service.ts` validates every response strictly (readings are three-decimal strings, a meter bill has both readings and no other bill has any) and returns `MeterWrite {readingId, meter, replayed}` for writes. Bills and receipts parse their `electricityMode` and readings through its `parseElectricity`.
- **Store:** `stores/meterReading.ts` keeps each tenant's meter as the backend last returned it (`meterFor`, `loadMeter`, `forgetMeter`); a failed load is an error with Retry, never an empty meter. Writes show only the meter the backend returns; the record key (per tenant) and correction key (per reading) are kept after an uncertain failure, so a retry is recognised, but only for the same input (changed values get a new key); an `idempotency_conflict` reloads the meter. `MeterReadingError.blockingEntries` lists the bills a `reading_in_use` names. It resets on a property change.
- **Bill form** (`RentEntryForm`, wired in `NewRentEntryView`): an "Include electricity charges" switch. With a baseline it loads the meter and shows the opening (last billed) reading, the units and the charge. When readings are already recorded and no bill closes on them (`utils/meterBilling.ts` `unbilledReadings`: the active ones after the last billed, e.g. October's after its bill was voided, even with later ones recorded), the bill **closes on one by default** (the one dated in the billing month, else the newest; a picker appears when there are several) and sends `closingReadingId`; **Record a new reading instead** switches to a typed closing reading (or, with the **Record meter reading** switch turned off: `ReadingEntryToggle`, on by default, the left half beside "Include electricity charges" for every meter tenant; turning it off enters a new reading even when a recorded one was chosen, and **Use a recorded reading** turns it back on, the units used: "The reading becomes 2,985" previews `utils/meterReadingValidation.ts` `readingFromUnits` on the newest reading, `validateUnitsDraft` dates them after it, and the bill sends `closingReading.units`) and its date (the initial payment's date when a reading can be dated then, else today, until edited), checked against the newest active reading, not only the last billed one. Without a baseline, typed units as before. Off: "Not charged". The confirmation shows the backend's figures (`utils/electricity.ts`: "Electricity (2,563 → 2,985: 422 units × ₹8)", or "Not charged", never ₹0). After a bill with a reading, the meter is forgotten so the next page loads it afresh.
- **Bills:** the latest bill card, the bill details (opening and closing readings with their dates) and the receipt (`utils/receiptContent.ts`: an "Electricity charge (units × rate per unit)" row, and for a meter bill an **Electricity consumption details** block with the opening and closing readings, units and rate, and when each was taken ("the starting reading" when the opening is the baseline, which has no date); or "Not charged" in the charges and the summary, never ₹0) show the saved mode and readings. `EntryCorrectionForm` keeps a meter or not-charged bill's electricity read-only and sends its units back unchanged.
- **Tenant:** `TenantDetails` shows **Meter** ("From 2,804", or "Not set up") and, once set up, a **Meter readings** button to `/tenants/:tenantId/meter` (`MeterReadingsView`): the starting and last billed readings, **Record reading** (the same switch: the reading or the units used), and `MeterReadingHistory` (consumption, "Entered as N units" for a reading entered as units, which bill each was billed on, void ones muted with why) with **Correct** and **Void** dialogs (`MeterReadingForm`, with no units option when correcting; `VoidReadingForm`). When the backend refuses because bills are measured from the reading, the dialog stays open and links every bill to void first, in order (newest first).

## API

Under `/api/v1/properties/{id}/tenancies/{id}/meter-readings/`, scoped through `properties_for_user` then the tenancy:

- `GET` → `{baseline, lastBilled, readings}`: readings newest first (void ones too), each `{id, reading, readOn, enteredUnits, notes, status, consumption, billedBy: {id, billingMonth} | null, createdAt, voidedAt, voidedByName, voidReason, replacesReadingId, replacedByReadingId}`. Unpaginated.
- `POST` `{reading | units, readOn, notes?}` with an Idempotency-Key: 201 `{reading: id, meter}`, 200 for a retry; 400 with the field that doesn't fit (`reading` when the meter isn't set up or neither is sent, `units` when both are, `readOn` for units not dated after the newest reading); 409 `duplicate_reading_day`, `idempotency_conflict`.
- `POST .../{readingId}/void/` `{reason}` and `POST .../{readingId}/correction/` `{reason, reading, readOn, notes?}` (Idempotency-Key): 409 `reading_in_use` (`entries`), `reading_void`, `reading_void_conflict`.

Migrations `billing.0013` (readings), `0014` (the readings' guard trigger), `0015` (the bills' and receipts' electricity mode and readings), `0016` (the bills' readings must belong to the bill's tenancy) and `0017` (`entered_units`, and the guard trigger refusing its edit) added these. Undoing `0017` drops the units entered (the readings stay). Undoing `0013` stops while any reading exists; undoing `0014` and `0016` only drop their triggers; undoing `0015`, while any meter or not-charged bill or such receipt exists.
